maia

One Maia

The rules every screen follows, agreed on 4 October 2026: one font, fourteen colours, six text sizes, one icon set, one logo and one word for each thing. Samples switch between English and Greek, because both must fit.

Words

One word for each thing

  • Tabs, titles, headings, tiles and links are nouns. A screen's title is the name she tapped.
  • Buttons are verbs: Save, Add task, Delete task, Start my journey.
  • Messages say what happened: "Maia can't open your journey", "A newer Maia is needed".
  • Every Greek string says what its English says.

Colour

Fourteen colours

Three families of three, five neutrals. Each family keeps one meaning. Text on a light step uses the dark step of the same family. Transparency is allowed; new colours are not.

Doctor and remindersΓιατρός και υπενθυμίσεις
  • Long words wrap rather than shrink; cards grow with her text size.
  • The bar's five names are the one exception: they share one size and shrink together.
  • Journey's week headings ("Week 25") are the Label size in Pine; their days are Caption in Muted.
  • Bold for titles, headings, labels and buttons; Regular for reading; Medium for a caption that needs weight.
  • The 161 sizes once typed into screens each moved to their role here; a test now keeps sizes out of screens.
